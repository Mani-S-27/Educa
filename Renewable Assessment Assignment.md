**From Bureaucrats to Space Diplomats: The Political Science Career Spectrum**


In this project, we are going to identify the current jobs, emerging jobs, upcoming jobs, and far future jobs in the field of Political Science, and what would be the subject combination required for the same. Does your school provide these subjects ? If not, suggest some subjects that can be provided for by the school. 
The rationale for designing this project is rooted in the need to guide students toward informed and purposeful subject choices. While the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and various school boards provide flexibility in subject selection, students often lack clarity on how to align these choices with long term academic and career goals. This gap frequently results in decisions based on peer influence or perceived prestige rather than sound reasoning.
Social Science disciplines such as Political Science and History are often undervalued in comparison to Science subjects, despite offering diverse and impactful career pathways. By engaging students in the process of identifying current jobs, emerging jobs, upcoming jobs, and far future jobs in the field of Political Science, this task aims to sensitise learners to the relevance and importance of these subjects in shaping society and governance.

# Political Science Career Pathways 

## **CURRENT JOBS**  
**Definition:** Established, traditional roles in governance, public administration, and social sciences with stable demand and clear academic pathways.

### 1. Civil Servant / Bureaucrat  
- **Job Description:** Works in government administration, policy implementation, public service delivery.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + History + Economics  

### 2. Legislative Assistant  
- **Job Description:** Supports lawmakers with research, drafting, and legislative processes.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Law + Language  

### 3. Political Analyst / Policy Analyst  
- **Job Description:** Studies political trends, policies, and governance systems.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Economics + Statistics  

### 4. Teacher / Lecturer (Political Science)  
- **Job Description:** Teaches civics, political theory, governance.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + History + Education  

### 5. NGO Program Officer  
- **Job Description:** Designs and manages social development programs.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Sociology + Public Administration  

### 6. Public Relations Officer  
- **Job Description:** Manages communication for political or public institutions.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + English + Media Studies  

## **EMERGING JOBS**  
**Definition:** New roles gaining prominence due to digital governance, behavioural research, and data‑driven policy systems.

### 1. Tech Policy Specialist  
- **Job Description:** Designs policies for digital governance, data protection, AI regulation.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Computer Science + Law  

### 2. Political Behaviour Analyst  
- **Job Description:** Studies voter psychology, behavioural trends, political attitudes.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Psychology + Statistics  

### 3. Digital Campaign Strategist  
- **Job Description:** Designs online political campaigns using data analytics.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Media Studies + Data Science  

### 4. Cybersecurity Policy Advisor  
- **Job Description:** Creates policies for digital safety, cyber law, national security.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Cybersecurity + Law  

### 5. Social Media Policy Consultant  
- **Job Description:** Advises institutions on digital ethics, misinformation, platform governance.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Media Studies + Ethics  

### 6. Open Data Governance Specialist  
- **Job Description:** Works on transparency, public data systems, and digital accountability.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Data Science + Public Administration  

## **UPCOMING JOBS (Next 5–10 Years)**

### 1. E‑Voting Infrastructure Designer

### 2. Refugee Integration Systems Designer

### 3. Digital Identity Systems Architect

### 5. AI Bias Mitigation Specialist

- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + AI/Data Science + Ethics  

### 6. Humanitarian Technology Coordinator  
- **Job Description:** Uses technology for disaster relief and humanitarian aid.  
- **Subject Combination:** Political Science + Sociology + Information Technology

# 📘 School Readiness Rubric for Future‑Oriented Career Pathways  

This rubric helps schools evaluate how well they prepare students for current, emerging, upcoming, and far‑future careers in Political Science and related interdisciplinary fields.

## **1. Alignment with Current Job Pathways**  
**Beginner:**  
- Offers only traditional Social Science subjects  
- Students aware only of conventional roles  

**Proficient:**  
- Provides multiple Social Science subjects  
- Students exposed to a wider range of current careers  

**Advanced:**  
- Strong subject combinations  
- Real‑world exposure  
- Partnerships with governance/policy bodies  

## **2. Alignment with Emerging Job Pathways**  
**Beginner:**  
- Emerging careers not discussed  
- No interdisciplinary links with technology or data  

**Proficient:**  
- Relevant subjects exist (Computer Science, Psychology)  
- Occasional interdisciplinary integration  

**Advanced:**  
- Clear interdisciplinary pathways  
- Students engage in civic‑tech, behavioural analysis, and digital governance projects  

## **3. Alignment with Upcoming Job Pathways (5–10 Years)**  
**Beginner:**  
- No curriculum or guidance related to future governance or AI‑linked roles  

**Proficient:**  
- Some exposure to AI ethics, digital citizenship, or migration systems  

**Advanced:**  
- Active preparation through modules, simulations, and interdisciplinary projects  
- Alignment with upcoming roles  

## **4. Alignment with Far‑Future Job Pathways (Speculative)**  
**Beginner:**  
- No engagement with speculative or futuristic careers  

**Proficient:**  
- Occasional activities exploring speculative governance (debates, model‑UN)  

**Advanced:**  
- Futures thinking embedded  
- Students work on speculative governance challenges  
- Hybrid subject pathways encouraged  

## **5. Interdisciplinary Strength**  
**Beginner:**  
- Subjects taught in silos  
- Minimal cross‑department collaboration  

**Proficient:**  
- Some interdisciplinary projects  
- Teachers recognise cross‑subject relevance  

**Advanced:**  
- Interdisciplinary learning embedded  
- Teachers co‑design units  
- Students choose interdisciplinary pathways  

(Note: The core conceptual inspiration for this work is credited to the Nature Nurture Learning Ecosystem.While the original platform briefly touched upon multiple subjects and associated soft skills, this project deliberately narrows the focus to Political Science as a specialised niche and extends the idea by examining subject specific combinations rather than soft skill development.
